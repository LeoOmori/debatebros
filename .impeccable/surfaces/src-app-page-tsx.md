# Phase 2 — fluxo navegável

Mode: Operate. Audience: curious Portuguese-speaking learners. Job: configure and finish a structured mock debate, then understand the review. Scope: landing, setup, room, result, responsive and recovery states. No live AI, accounts, custom themes or cloud history.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A focused live broadcast where the agenda, right to speak and listening judge make debate structure visible; refuse generic chat.

OWN-WORLD: Ink-black studio field, chalk-white reading surfaces, vermilion tally lights, signal green completion and periwinkle judge cues; squared captions, rails and broadcast lower-thirds rather than cards.

STORY: Choose the cast and motion, go on air, answer three bounded turns, then receive an evidenced review.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A horizontal debate desk fills the frame: opponent left, active speech large at center, participant and judge right, with the six-stage rundown below and the next action anchored at its edge.

FORM: Mesa de Transmissão, grounded candidate 1, seed 17419e46.
