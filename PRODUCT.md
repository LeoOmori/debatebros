# debatebros

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS. Google Gemini is integrated through the Vercel AI SDK and configured server-side. The balanced defaults use a Flash model for turns and a stronger Pro model for judgment.

## Users

Portuguese-speaking curious learners who want an accessible, entertaining way to exercise argumentation and understand major ideas without requiring formal philosophy training.

## Product Purpose

debatebros lets a person choose a historical opponent, a judge, a curated thesis and a side, complete a structured debate, and receive an evidence-based review. MVP success means people finish the debate and find the final evaluation useful enough to begin another one.

## Positioning

Unlike an open-ended character chatbot, debatebros enforces a balanced sequence of opening, argument, rebuttal, counter-rebuttal, conclusion and independent judgment. The product teaches through active opposition and transcript-based feedback.

## Operating Context

The experience is a short, focused solo session on desktop or mobile. The participant reads one argument at a time, writes three bounded responses, follows visible debate progress and receives a final review. No account is required.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Portuguese only in the MVP.
- Opponents: Nietzsche, Kant, Freud and Jung.
- Judges: Socrates, Aristotle and Hannah Arendt.
- Phase 2 uses only the eight curated themes; custom theses are deferred.
- No authentication, payments, cloud history, voice, multiplayer, ranking or open-ended chat.
- The current session and latest result will be stored in the browser.
- Historical figures must be labeled as educational simulations.
- Unsourced generated text must never be presented as a literal quotation.

## Brand Commitments

The confirmed product name is `debatebros`. No logo, visual identity, external reference or marketing claim has been supplied.

## Evidence on Hand

The project contains typed starter catalogs, brief bibliographies and a tested debate state machine. It has no testimonials, usage data, original portraits, verified quotations or commercial claims; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Structure before spectacle: every screen makes the current stage and expected action clear.
- Teach through friction: opponents engage the participant's actual argument rather than agreeing reflexively.
- Explain judgment: every score is justified from the transcript.
- Historical humility: simulations are useful interpretations, not authentic resurrections.
- Finishable by design: bounded turns and visible progress favor completed debates over endless conversation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web experience must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks, readable contrast, reduced motion and responsive use at a 390 px viewport.
